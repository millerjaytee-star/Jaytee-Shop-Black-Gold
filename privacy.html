Privacy

Stabilis Ops Group, LLC operates Stabilis Operator Intelligence™. This notice covers the website, the iOS app and invited customer workspaces.

Accounts and access

Accounts are issued by invitation for an organization workspace. Stabilis uses your work email, authentication information, role and location assignments to sign you in and limit access to authorized records. Supabase provides authentication and customer data storage. The iOS app stores sign-in session credentials in the device’s Keychain.

Operating records and evidence

Depending on your pilot scope, the workspace stores sales, staffing schedules, paid labor, inventory, purchasing, food costs, location P&L and other records needed for the agreed diagnostic. Review records include assigned people, decisions, timestamps, comparison data and evidence notes. These records support validation, variance calculation, findings, action follow-through and approved reporting. Access follows organization and location grants.

Submit operating records only through the authorized customer intake under your pilot agreement. The iOS app uses scoped review summaries and notes. Original uploads follow the signed scope, including any restricted-folder and retention provisions.

Website, demo and support

The public walkthrough uses fictional café records. The app saves fictional walkthrough progress on your device so you can return to it. The web demo and Control Room can store local progress or inputs in your browser.

Netlify hosts the website and processes fit-call and support requests. Stabilis uses the form contact details and message to respond. The customer web workspace may record usage events such as workspace views. The iOS app has no advertising, third-party analytics or push notifications.

Retention, requests and deletion

Customer operating data and raw files are retained under the applicable pilot agreement. Stabilis may retain information it needs for a legal, security or contractual obligation. Stabilis does not sell account, support or operating data to advertisers. Service providers process data needed to operate the service.

Use Stabilis support to request access, correction or deletion. For account deletion, choose the account deletion option using your work email. Stabilis will verify your identity before processing the request and explain which shared workspace records need to be retained.
