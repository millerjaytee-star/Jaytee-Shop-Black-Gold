Customer data privacy

Privacy

Current controlled-pilot data practices: customer operating data remains tenant-scoped and is used for the authorized Stabilis workflow.

Data used

Depending on pilot scope, Stabilis may use sales, labor, schedules, inventory, purchasing, food cost, P&L, locations, targets and management context required for the agreed diagnostic.

Tenant isolation

Production customer data is stored inside the customer’s organization boundary. Harbor & Hearth is fictional and never substitutes for real customer analytics.

Purpose

Data supports validation, normalization, deterministic metrics, evidence-backed findings, approved recommendations, actions, authorized reports and result measurement.

Telemetry

General product usage events capture product behavior such as workspace views rather than copying raw sensitive financial records.

Retention

Real pilot retention and deletion scope should be documented in the customer agreement before sensitive uploads begin.

Sharing

Stabilis does not sell customer conversation or operating data to advertisers. Service providers may process data only as needed to operate the service.
