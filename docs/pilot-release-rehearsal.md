## Synthetic reviewer walkthrough

Record the branch, commit, web origin, iPhone build, device, OS, test time and result. Use fictional records in the public demo, simulator and isolated staging. Keep account credentials in the approved private reviewer fields. Use an isolated browser profile and record any test artifact that needs deletion. At $9,000 net sales and $2,700 paid labor, actual labor is 30%, five points above target, and paid hours are 10 above schedule. The $450 historical variance is $0 from the schedule, $250 from sales below forecast and $200 from payroll above schedule. |

## Operator pilot session

Use this script after the founder authorizes participation and the approved environment and engagement permit the records. The 14-day diagnostic starts on the agreed date after signed scope, cleared payment in full and minimum data readiness. Confirm the one location, its fiscal week, the approved folder and the raw-file retention date. [Pilot scope and research plan](pilot-operating-model.md) · [Security release gate](security-release-gate.md)

1. **Set context.** Ask “Tell me about the last time this location missed its labor plan. Which records did you use? What took time? What decision did you make, and who followed up?” Record the operator’s normal workflow and any report or decision they could not reconcile.
2. **Observe the incumbent.** Have the operator reproduce the review in the tools they already use, such as Restaurant365 or Homebase. Record the settings and reports they use. Identify the export version, the first time the operator can explain the variance correctly using sources, and any remaining uncertainty. Record who does the work and the minutes spent by role.
3. **Run the Stabilis review on the same week.** Use the same location, approved measures, source versions and decision. Start the timing clock when all usable exports are ready. Ask the operator to explain the contribution from schedule, sales and payroll before suggesting a cause. Note any prior exposure to the answer or differences in inputs, permissions or configuration that could affect the comparison.
4. **Observe the handoff.** Ask the owner to select an action with a named owner, measure, due date, evidence and next review. Have the GM open the assignment, explain the task back, accept or request a change and identify the evidence they would submit. Record any help required.
5. **Review the following period.** Check the owner’s return, action acceptance and completion dates, comparison quality and whether the evidence changed the decision. Record any request for another cycle. If the operator reaches an equivalent decision with its existing configured tools, record the time, effort and quality trade-offs and consider implementation support.

## Private measurement schema

Keep the log in the founder’s approved restricted folder. Use pseudonymous pilot, location, action and review IDs and link only to authorized supporting records. Enter actual observations separately from research targets. Leave unavailable measures blank and record the reason. [First-month targets](pilot-operating-model.md#first-month-research-plan)

| Record | Required fields and measurement rule |
|---|---|
| Session | Pilot ID, location ID, fiscal week, method, source versions, reviewer role, UTC start, local timezone, tool settings, prior exposure, comparable inputs, outcome and evidence references. Keep one row per method and period. |
| Timing | Usable exports ready and first correct, sourced explanation timestamps. Elapsed minutes = time between them, including pauses. Record preparation, analyst, support and manager active minutes separately with a pause reason where applicable. If the explanation never becomes supportable, leave its timestamp blank and record the blocker. |
| Decision and evidence | Variance explanation, measured components, unresolved cause, decision, proposed action, evidence that changed the decision and operator agreement. Record historical variance, modeled opportunity, observed change and client-verified impact separately. Attach the eligible finance reference and period adjustments only when they exist. |
| Follow-through | Review and action IDs, assignee role, assignment, original and revised due dates, acceptance or change request, completion and evidence timestamps, next review, owner return and repeat-cycle request. Report acceptance and completion by due date over actions due in the measured period. Record approved due-date changes and count incomplete due actions in the denominator. |
| Commercial and research | Inquiry, signed proposal, cleared payment and kickoff as separate events with source and timestamp. Count paid pilots using cleared payment. Store research week, target, actual, source and reason for any gap separately. |
| Delivery and correction | Signed scope ID, agreed recipients, readout timestamp, exported version and restricted file reference, received correction date, disposition, corrected version and delivery date. Record storage cutoff and permitted retained audit references. |

## Readout and factual correction

At the 60-minute readout, cover the sourced baseline and gaps, the labor bridge and inventory evidence, supported low-to-base opportunities with overlap removed, up to five owned actions and the 90-day roadmap. Deliver the agreed version through the restricted method in the signed scope. Record the delivery and retain or remove originals under that scope. Record one factual correction pass requested within five business days under the agreed terms and keep the version history. Scope implementation and ongoing reviews separately.
