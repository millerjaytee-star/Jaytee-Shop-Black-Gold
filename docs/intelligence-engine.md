# Intelligence Engine

Financial truth is deterministic and versioned.

Raw records → normalized facts → deterministic metrics → approved benchmarks → findings → quantified opportunities → canonical deduplication → priority → recommendation candidate → analyst review → action → result verification.

## Deterministic responsibilities
Net sales, labor %, COGS %, prime cost, SPLH, average check, budget/prior-year variance, inventory variance, scoring, opportunity math, recoverability ranges, priority math and rollups are code/SQL responsibilities.

## AI responsibilities
Narrative synthesis, explanation, executive summary drafting, pattern surfacing and recommendation drafting are allowed only from authorized structured facts. Generated narratives are stored separately with provider/model/version when practical.

## Confidence
Confidence inherits the weakest material evidence layer: input quality, calculation validity, benchmark quality, causal certainty and freshness. Missing data is never silently replaced with invented certainty.

## Opportunity contract
Every counted opportunity stores a canonical ID, category, baseline/target, source metrics, calculation version, low/base/high modeled range, confidence, evidence and analyst state. Related evidence may support the same root issue without increasing the enterprise total.
