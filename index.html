14-Day Margin Recovery Diagnostic

$1,500Fixed fee · One agreed location

Days 1–5

Establish the baseline

We check eight complete weeks of daily net sales and scheduled and paid labor by role. We match opening and closing counts and purchases to one closed four-week inventory period and review available waste records and the latest location P&L.

Days 6–10

Explain the difference

We trace findings back to records, model a low-to-base opportunity where the evidence supports it, and remove overlaps before totaling the estimates.

Days 11–14

Agree on the next move

You leave a 60-minute readout with up to five prioritized actions, named owners and measures, and a 90-day roadmap your team can use.

The 14 calendar days start on the agreed date after signed scope, cleared payment in full and minimum data readiness.
