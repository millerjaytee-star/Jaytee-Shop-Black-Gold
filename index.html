What we diagnose

Your P&L shows the result. We trace the operating cause.

We do not assume a business is broken from the outside. We use operating data, observation, leadership context, and process evidence to test where control may be weakening.

01

Labor & Productivity

Scheduling, deployment, overtime, throughput, role clarity, and whether labor hours are producing the intended operating output.

02

Food Cost, Inventory & Waste

Ordering, receiving, pars, transfers, counts, waste, remakes, variance review, and the management loop behind the numbers.

03

Purchasing & Controls

Vendor discipline, invoice control, quantity/price exceptions, ownership, and repeatable purchasing routines.

04

Execution Consistency

Open, peak, handoff, close, service standards, takeout/delivery flow, and whether different leaders run the same standard the same way.

05

Leadership & Accountability

Manager scorecards, coaching cadence, action ownership, escalation, verification, and whether problems actually close.

06

Operating Visibility

KPI rhythm, exception reporting, multi-unit consistency, growth readiness, and whether senior leaders can see where intervention is needed.
