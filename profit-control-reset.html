30-Day Profit Control Reset™

After a diagnostic identifies an operating issue, the Reset helps your managers put the agreed change into daily use. Scope, timing and fee are agreed separately.

What the four weeks cover

  1. Week 1 · Establish the baselineAgree on the specific control, the records that show the gap and the measure to follow.
  2. Week 2 · Assign ownershipName the manager responsible, set a due date and agree on evidence of completion.
  3. Week 3 · Run the reviewUse shift huddles, the weekly scorecard and an exception log to catch changes in time to act.
  4. Week 4 · Check the resultReview the evidence and assign the next 30-, 60- and 90-day priorities.

Stabilis draws on MarginControl OS™ for financial controls, LeaderOS™ for manager ownership and SafeShift OS™ for shift routines. The operating team receives the scorecard, exception log, control-gap map and action plan agreed in the implementation scope.

The $1,500 founding pilot is a 14-Day Margin Recovery Diagnostic at one location. On the fit call, we review the operating concern and data readiness. You decide whether to pursue implementation after reviewing the findings.
