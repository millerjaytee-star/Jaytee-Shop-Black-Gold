How the numbers work

Operating contribution
Net sales − loaded labor − COGS − other operating expenses. Not net income, EBITDA, or a complete accounting statement.
Modeled target gap
Positive labor gap + positive COGS gap. Overlapping waste, overtime, refunds and revenue findings are not added. No annualization or guaranteed recovery.
Inventory usage
Opening inventory + received purchases + transfers in − transfers out − closing inventory. Enter explicit zeros where appropriate.
Cash projection
Available cash + expected next-period receipts − all next-period cash commitments. This is your cash scenario, not a bank balance.
Observed intervention result
Baseline cost rate × post-period sales − post-period cost − implementation spend. Equal period lengths are required; mix, timing and outside factors can still explain changes.

Live POS sync, team accounts, automated purchasing/payroll, regulatory certification and independently verified savings are not supplied by this workspace. Existing controlled-pilot services remain separate.
